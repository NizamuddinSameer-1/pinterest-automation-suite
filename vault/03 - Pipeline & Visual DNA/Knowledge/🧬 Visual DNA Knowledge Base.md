# 🧬 Visual DNA Knowledge Base

Visual DNA is the extracted mathematical and stylistic representation of what makes a real photograph feel **authentic, spontaneous, and believable** rather than synthetic or over-polished.

---

## 📐 The 7 DNA Dimensions

### 1. Capture Identity (`capture_identity`)
- **Type:** `casual_ugc_smartphone` | `styled_flat_lay` | `mirror_selfie` | `discovery_snapshot`
- **Professionalism:** `very_low` to `high` *(UGC sweet spot: `very_low` to `low`)*
- **Spontaneity:** `low` to `very_high` *(Viral Pinterest sweet spot: `high`)*

### 2. Composition DNA (`composition_dna`)
- **Centering:** `centered` | `slightly_off_center` | `off_center` *(Human framing is rarely centered)*
- **Framing:** `imperfect` | `natural` | `tight`
- **Camera Height:** `human_standing` | `sitting` | `floor_level` | `eye_level`

### 3. Environment DNA (`environment_dna`)
- **Real-World Context:** `true` (Always true for UGC)
- **Clutter Level:** `moderate` to `high` (Racks, hangers, shopping carts, floor textures)
- **Background Activity:** Store aisles, hallway corners, living room rugs

### 4. Lighting DNA (`lighting_dna`)
- **Source:** `retail_fluorescent` | `natural_window` | `indoor_mixed` | `diffuse_daylight`
- **Contrast:** `low` to `moderate` (Avoid dramatic cinematic Rembrandt shadows)
- **Warmth:** `neutral_to_cool` for retail, `warm_neutral` for cozy home scenes

### 5. Camera DNA (`camera_dna`)
- **Device Behavior:** `smartphone_behavior: true`
- **Sharpness:** `natural_smartphone` (Avoid 8K tack-sharp macro lens look)
- **Noise:** `subtle_sensor_grain` (Especially in indoor ambient light)
- **HDR:** `restrained` (No neon halo effect around highlights)

### 6. Material DNA (`material_dna`)
- **Texture Visibility:** `high` (Fleece fibers, fabric wrinkles, nail polish gloss, skin pores)
- **Surface Imperfection:** `moderate` (Believable fabric fold compression, natural reflections)

### 7. Realism Markers (`realism_markers`)
- **Anti-Studio:** `true` (Strips white cyclorama, studio strobes, beauty dishes)
- **Anti-Cinematic:** `true` (Strips movie grading, anamorphic flares, teal & orange tint)
